# Figma — Website Study

## Snapshot
- Product category: collaborative design platform
- Design archetype: playful modernism with strong product storytelling
- Best used for: design tools, collaborative software, creative productivity

## Observed design language
- Large type, bold image scale, and intentionally varied compositions.
- Product UI appears as proof, but brand graphics and expressive art direction are also important.
- Strong contrast between structured product information and more playful campaign moments.
- Layout often uses oversized sections and deliberate visual interruptions rather than one repeated card system.
- Color is expressive but tied to narrative moments instead of every control.

## Why it works
The site can feel creative without losing product clarity. It demonstrates that a modern product site does not need to choose between expressive brand and functional software evidence.

## Reusable rules
```yaml
layout:
  composition: varied
  section_scale: large
  grid_breaks: intentional

typography:
  display: bold
  hierarchy: high-contrast

color:
  role: narrative-accent

composition:
  combine_brand_art_and_product_ui: true
```

## Good use cases
- creative tools
- collaborative products
- portfolio storytelling
- product launches with stronger brand expression

## Do not copy blindly
High visual variety requires strong art direction; without it, varied layouts become inconsistency rather than rhythm.
