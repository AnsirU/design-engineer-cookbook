# Framer — Website Study

## Snapshot
- Product category: website builder / creative tool
- Design archetype: expressive minimalism with strong motion and type
- Best used for: creative tools, design products, portfolio inspiration

## Observed design language
- Large typography and dramatic composition establish hierarchy quickly.
- Motion, transitions, and interactive examples are central to storytelling.
- Product demonstrations often become the section layout itself.
- Color is more expressive than in typical B2B SaaS, but surfaces remain controlled.
- Grid is frequently broken intentionally to create energy.

## Why it works
Framer demonstrates the medium while marketing the product: the website itself becomes proof of capability.

## Reusable rules
```yaml
layout:
  grid: structured-with-controlled-breaks
  product_demo: integrated

typography:
  display: dominant
  hierarchy: dramatic

motion:
  role: product-proof
  intensity: medium-high

composition:
  use_interactive_demo_as_content: true
```

## Good use cases
- design tools
- creative AI products
- portfolios
- interactive launch pages

## Do not copy blindly
High motion intensity is inappropriate for dense analytical reports or task-heavy enterprise tools unless reduced significantly.
