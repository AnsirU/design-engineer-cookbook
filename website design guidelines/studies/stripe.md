# Stripe — Website Study

## Snapshot
- Product category: payments / financial infrastructure
- Design archetype: premium technical storytelling with expressive gradients
- Best used for: fintech, platform products, complex B2B storytelling

## Observed design language
- Strong editorial hierarchy with large type, dense product proof, and carefully staged sections.
- Uses gradients and color fields as brand devices while keeping text and core UI highly structured.
- Product screenshots, diagrams, metrics, and code frequently serve as evidence for claims.
- Sections vary in composition to avoid repetitive SaaS-card rhythm.
- Complex content is made approachable through progressive disclosure and clear grouping.

## Why it works
Stripe balances trust, technical depth, and visual distinction. The page can carry substantial information without looking like a dashboard.

## Reusable rules
```yaml
layout:
  storytelling: editorial
  section_composition: varied
  proof_density: high

typography:
  display: strong
  body_measure: controlled

color:
  brand_expression: selective-gradient
  content_surfaces: restrained

composition:
  mix: [product-ui, diagrams, metrics, code]
```

## Good use cases
- fintech
- platform products
- enterprise product launches
- complex product portfolios

## Do not copy blindly
Expressive gradients are part of Stripe's brand language; use the compositional logic without automatically copying the visual skin.
