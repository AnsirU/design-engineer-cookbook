# Notion — Website Study

## Snapshot
- Product category: productivity / knowledge work
- Design archetype: warm minimalism with editorial illustration and product proof
- Best used for: productivity, collaboration, knowledge tools

## Observed design language
- Light, warm neutral surfaces with restrained black typography.
- Product UI screenshots are mixed with illustration and editorial framing.
- Typography is large but friendly rather than technical or severe.
- Section structure is simple, with strong visual pauses and relatively little chrome.
- Visual hierarchy often depends on scale and whitespace instead of card stacking.

## Why it works
The site makes a complex workspace product feel approachable without hiding its capability.

## Reusable rules
```yaml
layout:
  shell: light-editorial
  section_spacing: generous

typography:
  tone: warm-direct
  display: large

surface:
  cards: selective
  borders: minimal

composition:
  combine_product_ui_and_illustration: true
```

## Good use cases
- collaboration tools
- knowledge products
- team software
- approachable B2B storytelling

## Do not copy blindly
The warm editorial tone is less appropriate for highly technical or operations-heavy enterprise products.
