07
FRAMEWORK / FLOW
DESIGN STRATEGY

Three rules shaped every design decision.

01State

Expose state before detail.

Show what matters now. Keep logs and implementation detail one layer deeper.

02Control

Intervention belongs in the workflow.

Pause, edit plan, review change and retry should never feel like error handling.

03Evidence

Every result needs proof.

Connect output to tests, diffs or observable evidence before asking users to trust it.
